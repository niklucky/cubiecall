  1. A face is that person's video switch. Click yours and your camera starts; click it again and it stops. Click Anna's and her video hides; again and it's back. Press ▶ Show me to watch it happen.
  2. Drag the pill to the left or right edge. Pause there and you see the column it will become; let go and it turns. Take the column to the top or bottom edge and it lies down again. In the middle of the screen it keeps its shape.
  3. Push it right into an edge (the last few pixels) and it tucks away as a tab with a dot per person: green while they speak, red for you muted. Click the tab to bring the call back.
  4. Mute, then hold the mic button to talk; let go and you're muted again. Space does the same while the cursor is over this desktop. Talk while muted plays the reminder: a note on the mic and one soft beep, here at most every 20 s (5 minutes in the app).
  5. Transcribing: On shows the caption line. Bubbles ride above it.
  6. Double-click an empty spot of the pill for the call window. Right-click it for the menu as macOS draws it.
  7. Bubbles: drag them, scroll over one to zoom, double-click for big or small. Drop one near the pill and it rides again.
