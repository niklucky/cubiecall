Quipcall mobile · round 1

The iPhone app, drawn before it's built (Android follows the same screens). Telegram is the model: one fast list, a face on every row, nothing on screen that doesn't earn its place. Dark only, the desktop's colours. Tap the round call buttons to see their states.

Since then (2026-11-16): the app uses the system's own components and follows the phone's light or dark, and has four tabs (Contacts, Calls, Rooms, Settings) instead of this board's mixed list. See docs/UI.md. The screens not built yet still stand as drawn.

1 · Calls: rooms and people in one list

Telegram's chat list, for calls. A room is a group; a person is a personal call. Whatever is live sits on top with a green Join. Everything else is ordered by the last call.

2 · The call

Full screen, video edge to edge, five buttons. A white button is on and worth noticing (the speaker is loud, you're muted); grey is the quiet state. Minimise it and the call rides on the app as the desktop's pill.

3 · Getting in, and settings

To decide

  • One list or two? Drawn: rooms and people together under "Calls", as Telegram mixes groups and people. The other way is a Rooms tab with rooms only, and people only under Contacts.
  • A room opens, or calls? Drawn: a tap opens the room (its calls, later its transcripts) and Join is a second tap, except on a live room's green button. The built spike calls on the first tap.
  • Personal calls need two things we don't have yet: contacts and ringing on the server (plan Stages 3–5), and an Apple Developer account for call notifications (VoIP push) and TestFlight.
